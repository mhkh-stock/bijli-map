Aapke district ki load-shedding schedule

Official load-shedding schedules for your district and grid station, published by the government admin — plus on/off messages from people in your own grid only. Every person sees only their own district.

🔒 Login required. Sign up with your Gmail and CNIC — your CNIC district code must match the district you select. Schedules and messages are never shown to another district. For an official complaint, call the national electricity helpline 118.

CNIC check, honestly: we check that the first 3 digits of your CNIC match your district's prefix. This is a format/prefix check only — it is not NADRA verification, and it cannot prove the CNIC belongs to you. A CNIC also records where it was registered, not where you live now, so a real resident can hold another district's prefix. Your full CNIC is never stored — only a one-way hash of it.

How it works

  1. Sign up with Gmail, password and CNIC. Your CNIC district must match the district you pick.
  2. Open your dashboard — you see only your district.
  3. Pick your grid station — a grid is shown only after the admin publishes a schedule for it.
  4. See the official schedule for your grid, and post "power on / power off" so your neighbours in the same grid know.

Government admins publish the schedule from the admin portal. Admin accounts are created by the master admin only — there is no self-registration for admins. An admin can publish, edit and delete the schedule for their own grid only, never another district's.

DISCO directory — all of Pakistan

Pakistan has 10 public distribution companies plus K-Electric for Karachi. Load-shedding is decided per grid station / feeder (the line from the grid station), not per neighbourhood — two nearby streets can have different schedules. Your feeder name is on your bill.
