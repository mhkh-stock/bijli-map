Scheduled vs actual — kitni der light gayi?

Scheduled hours are the official schedule entered by admin (examples are marked EXAMPLE). Actual hours are computed only from completed out → restored report pairs on that date. An outage still open is not counted, and with few reports the actual figure is incomplete — the site says so instead of guessing.

UPS runtime estimator — kitni der chalega?

Battery energy (Ah × volts) × inverter efficiency ÷ load = runtime. Default efficiency 85% (inverter losses). Battery age, temperature and real load change the result — this is an estimate, not a promise.

Enter your battery and load, then press Estimate.

DISCO directory — all of Pakistan

Pakistan has 10 public distribution companies plus K-Electric for Karachi. Load-shedding is decided per feeder (the line from the grid station), not per neighbourhood — two nearby streets can have different schedules. Your feeder name is on your bill.
